About this dashboard

This dashboard maps C-HAWQ's statewide residential canal footprint — the network of artificial, residential waterways carved into Florida's coastlines. Each polygon is one mapped canal system with its area, perimeter, county, water management district, and basin planning attributes. Use the filters and the map together: pan and zoom to a community, click any canal for its details, and turn on "Limit stats to map view" to make the indicators and charts describe only what's on screen.

Data source

C-HAWQ Residential Canal Spatial Footprint (finalized, June 2026), derived from the Coastal Habitat and Water Quality Initiative's canal delineation pipeline. Geometry reprojected to WGS84 for web display and simplified for performance; areas and perimeters are computed from the source geometry. Attribute fields shown: county, waterbody name, water management district, basin planning unit, basin group, HUC-8, WBID, area, and perimeter.
