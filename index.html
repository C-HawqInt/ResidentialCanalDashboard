How to use: Filter with the dropdowns, the search box, or by clicking a bar in the charts below. The search box suggests cities, counties, canal names and any place in Florida as you type, and forgives typos and abbreviations (try “Ft Myers” or “Sanibal”). Click any canal on the map for its details and to add it to your request selection; added canals turn yellow and appear under “Selected for request” beside the map. Use “Clear selection” on the map (or click a highlighted list row again) to deselect a canal without touching your request list. The charts follow the county, district, and size filters only, not map panning; each has its own Reset. Recolor canals from the legend under the map, switch between map and satellite at the map's top-right, and use “Request shapefiles” to have your selection emailed to you. Reset clears everything and returns to the full statewide view.

About this dashboard

This dashboard maps C-HAWQ's statewide residential canal footprint — the network of artificial, residential waterways carved into Florida's coastlines. Each polygon is one mapped canal system with its source attributes, including typology and classification fields. Use the filters and the map together: pan and zoom to a community, click any canal for its details, and turn on "Limit stats to map view" to make the indicators and canal list describe only what's on screen. The charts summarize the canals matching the county, water management district, and size filters, and stay put as you pan and zoom.

Data source

Geometry and attributes are loaded from the ResCanals typology shapefile archive. Form (TYP_FORM), receiving waters (TYP_RECWATER), and connectivity (TYP_CONNEC) are available as filters and map color dimensions; all other source attributes are available in feature popups.

Notes

An internet connection is required for the basemap tiles and mapping libraries. Place search sends the text you type to the free Photon and Nominatim geocoding services (OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors), limited to Florida; canal, city and county matches run entirely in your browser.
